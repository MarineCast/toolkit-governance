GOVERNANCE REMAINING QUALIFICATION WORK
======================================

The production workflow is the offline flat-product build. Its application contract
is resources/app_contract.json; public usage, validation and refresh are documented
in ../../docs/parquet-products.md and ../../docs/coverage.md. Sixteen acquisition
collections feed seven presentation families. Native normalization is internal.

Remaining limitations require source evidence, not inferred geometry or dates:

- Coverage: bounded receipts do not establish complete geography. Preserve null
  no-hit support until independently qualified complete support exists.
- Legal time: current inventories do not reconstruct legal effective-date history.
  Keep provider publication/observation/update, retrieval and build times separate.
- Maritime support: Canadian internal-water, territorial-sea and contiguous-zone
  machine-readable authority remains incomplete; country/state assignments are
  geographic model-water references, not legal marine ownership.
- Fisheries: WDFW recreational, DFO subarea and ADF&G reporting systems remain
  distinct. Whole-area, other fisheries/species/gear systems and legal applicability
  need their own qualification; provider codes must not be pooled across systems.
- Indigenous references: BIA land-area and qualified NRCan reserve records do not
  establish traditional territory, sovereign jurisdiction, ownership or treaty rights.
- Critical habitat: DFO Final/Proposed stays unresolved. ECCC marine murrelet
  reference polygons require the stated biophysical conditions; polygon membership
  alone does not establish habitat everywhere or current legal protection.
- Conservation: unresolved cross-inventory site identity and marine eligibility
  require reviewed evidence. Proposed/interim assignment references remain excluded
  from designated count and union coverage.
- Restrictions: shoreline-dependent and moving zones, activity/vessel exceptions,
  current activation and legal-currentness gaps remain as documented in each
  DATA_SOURCES.md. No invented buffers, coastline closures or blanket bans.
- Routing: chart objects retain advisory/conditional roles; recorded presence does
  not establish mandatory status, current activation or vessel applicability.
- Rights: unresolved provider redistribution terms need source-specific clearance
  before sharing acquired datasets. Public endpoints do not imply permission.

For any future change, retain source identity/attributes/geometry, receipt scope,
units, denominator, null semantics and all overlaps; add focused tests, validate
an offline rebuild against retained originals, then run the full suite and strict
documentation build. See ../../docs/WORKFLOWS.md. Provider DATA_SOURCES.md files
remain the authority for precise gaps, prerequisites, citations and evidence.
