GOVERNANCE FEATURE-LAYER ROADMAP
================================

Current state
-------------

Phase 1 is in progress. Cross-layer infrastructure now lives in the explicit
``governance/shared`` package: native-geometry contracts, deterministic
``full_area`` clipping, acquisition/publication helpers, source/coverage
manifests, catalog validation, and map-layer descriptors. The governance root is
reserved for the consolidated inspector and package-level artifacts. Six
source-native collections now build: reconciled IBC/NOAA/NRCan international-
boundary references with derived country-water support, current NOAA dynamic
U.S. maritime-zone limits with the available NRCan/DFO-lineage Canadian EEZ
reference, the national BOEM state seaward boundary line, WDFW recreational
marine-area polygons, NOAA/DFO marine-protected-area references, and the complete
current NOAA West Coast national-marine-sanctuary roster with source-vintage GIS
polygons linked to controlling current CFR references. The single governance
inspector keeps source and jurisdiction delineations independently selectable
instead of flattening each collection into one map checkbox.

The release remains explicitly partial. Current public machine-readable
authoritative Canadian internal-water, territorial-sea, and contiguous-zone
geometry was not identified; these remain explicit unavailable states. PFMAs,
California/Oregon/Alaska fishery systems, and other federal/council management
areas are not yet acquired. No Phase 1 artifact should be interpreted as complete
full-Pacific legal coverage merely because it has been clipped to ``full_area``.

Priority labels are sequencing guidance:

* ``P0`` - shared contracts or time-varying layers directly relevant to SRKW
  analysis and operational maps.
* ``P1`` - important spatial context needed after the P0 contracts are stable.
* ``P2`` - useful administrative or conservation context that should not block
  the first governance release.


Definition of done for every feature family
-------------------------------------------

[ ] Add ``download.py``, ``build.py``, ``normalize.py``, ``inspect.py``,
    ``config.py``, and ``DATA_SOURCES.md`` in the leaf package, plus fixture-based
    tests. Source-specific interpretation belongs in the leaf normalizer; only
    contracts used by multiple feature families belong in ``governance/shared``.

[ ] Mirror the package path under ``config/data/governance/``,
    ``data/raw/governance/``, ``data/processed/domain/governance/``, and
    ``outputs/domains/governance/``.

[ ] Preserve two canonical products: source/normalized geometry and normalized
    legal/rule records. A mapped boundary is evidence about a rule; it is not
    itself the legal authority. Model-specific spatial projections are optional
    downstream artifacts, not governance feature-family deliverables.

[ ] Pin source URL/service layer, query, download time, source ``as_of`` time,
    checksum, license, attribution, legal citation, and redistribution terms in
    manifests. Archive each acquired snapshot instead of silently replacing it.

[ ] Represent both valid time (``EFFECTIVE_START`` and ``EFFECTIVE_END``) and
    system time (when the producer learned or published the record). Retain proposed,
    enacted, active, suspended, expired, repealed, and unknown states.

[ ] Keep ``mandatory``, ``voluntary``, and ``advisory`` rules distinct. Preserve
    regulating authority, regulated activity, vessel/gear/species applicability,
    exceptions, and source wording rather than collapsing them into a score.

[ ] Preserve native source geometry and publish a validated normalized geometry
    without coupling the governance artifact to a model grid or resolution.
    Record source coverage and geometry QC separately.

[ ] Keep source unavailability and unknown legal coverage explicit at the record
    and collection level. Only complete authoritative coverage may establish a
    true absence; an empty spatial join is not automatically false or zero.

[ ] Preserve overlapping authorities and designations as one-to-many records.
    Do not resolve overlap using an undocumented precedence rule or discard a
    lower-level authority merely because a federal polygon is present.

[ ] Validate CRS, geometry repair, duplicate IDs, effective-date intervals,
    legal-citation presence, cross-border coverage, source-to-normalized geometry
    conservation, temporal conflicts, and null/QC semantics.

[ ] Have each leaf ``inspect.py`` expose one or more reusable map-layer
    descriptors for source/normalized geometry, temporal state, source gaps, and
    legal-source links. Leaf inspectors do not write standalone maps by default;
    the root governance inspector composes them under the shared map contract.

[ ] Add an artifact-verified ``governance/feature_catalog.yaml`` and generator.
    Classify each field as predictor, state, evidence, coverage, provenance,
    support, or QC, with observed/derived/estimated/fallback/unavailable status.
    Mark all governance fields model-ineligible by default.

[ ] Add completed producers and catalog verification to
    the standalone ``governance build`` CLI only after each family can build
    from pinned local inputs without network access.

[ ] Perform temporal-leakage and circularity review before model eligibility.
    Critical habitat, SRKW recovery closures, and whale-protection zones may
    encode prior whale occurrence or policy response and are model-ineligible by
    default even when their geometry is static.


Shared full-AOI spatial scope - P0
----------------------------------

[ ] Preserve acquired raw files/services exactly as received. Spatially filter or
    clip every normalized point, line, and polygon product to ``full_area`` before
    publishing it under ``data/processed/domain/governance/``; raw archives are
    not destructively cropped.

[ ] Keep rules only when their linked applicability geometry or explicit legal
    scope intersects the full AOI. Preserve the complete rule text/record even
    when its mapped geometry is clipped, and prevent orphan geometry-to-rule or
    rule-to-authority relationships after slicing.

[ ] Fail validation when a normalized geometry lies outside ``full_area`` beyond
    the configured numerical tolerance, or when an in-scope source feature is
    dropped without a recorded exclusion reason. An empty in-AOI collection is
    valid only with explicit complete-source or unavailable-source evidence.


Shared governance inspection map - P0
-------------------------------------

[ ] Resolve time-varying layers for an explicit ``--as-of`` date before mapping.
    Display that date prominently and keep proposed, active, expired, and unknown
    states visually distinct. Never mix later rule corrections into an earlier
    inspection snapshot.

[ ] Base any split on recorded thresholds and a map manifest, not preference:
    test generated HTML size, load time, layer-toggle responsiveness, feature
    count, and legibility with representative overlapping selections. Keep one
    map when those checks pass.

[ ] Test that all implemented catalog layers are selectable, overlay selection is
    non-exclusive, category grouping and default visibility match config, tooltips
    render, temporal filtering honors ``--as-of``, missing layers remain explicit,
    output paths are canonical, and repeated builds are deterministic.


Shared governance contracts - P0
--------------------------------

[ ] Define a stable source-feature schema with ``GOVERNANCE_FEATURE_ID``,
    ``SOURCE_FEATURE_ID``, ``FEATURE_FAMILY``, ``FEATURE_TYPE``, ``NAME``,
    ``COUNTRY_CODE``, ``SUBDIVISION_CODE``, ``AUTHORITY``, ``LEGAL_CITATION``,
    ``LEGAL_STATUS``, ``BINDING_STATUS``, effective/system dates, source fields,
    coverage fields, QC fields, and geometry.

[ ] Define normalized many-to-many tables for geometry-to-authority,
    geometry-to-rule, rule-to-species, rule-to-gear, rule-to-vessel-class, and
    rule-to-exception relationships. Do not encode lists in opaque text columns.

[ ] Establish stable cross-border vocabularies without asserting false legal
    equivalence: U.S. federal/state/Tribal and Canadian federal/provincial/First
    Nations authorities retain their native meanings and source terminology.

[ ] Define source precedence by field, not by dataset. A regulatory text may be
    authoritative for dates and prohibitions while an agency GIS service is the
    preferred spatial representation; conflicts remain visible in QC.

[ ] Add a source inventory recording ``candidate``, ``approved``, ``blocked``,
    ``research_only``, or ``superseded`` status and the reason for that status.

[ ] Decide the temporal publication grain. Prefer change intervals and an
    as-of-date query API over dense daily copies. Any daily/weekly model panel is
    materialized downstream only for a declared experiment or application need.


Conditional modeling adapter - not a governance release requirement
-------------------------------------------------------------------


[ ] Promote a field to modeling only after documenting the ecological/operational
    mechanism, historical availability, source completeness, temporal leakage,
    policy-response circularity, and intended interpretation.

[ ] For an approved spatial covariate, project only the selected geometry to the
    experiment's configured model resolution and canonical marine support. Do
    not hard-code R8 or R6 in the governance producer.

[ ] Cache an optional sparse crosswalk containing ``GOVERNANCE_FEATURE_ID``,
    ``H3_INDEX``, ``H3_RESOLUTION``, spatial relation/overlap fields,
    ``SUPPORT_HASH``, geometry checksum, and projection method. The crosswalk is
    owned by feature/model preparation and is reproducible from native geometry.

[ ] Resolve time-varying rules as they were knowable at each training/forecast
    cutoff, then join the selected crosswalk to the model's H3-by-week panel.
    Never use a later correction or annual rule snapshot retrospectively.

[ ] Keep administrative IDs, reporting regions, critical habitat, MPAs, and
    whale-protection areas out of the default predictor matrix. Shipping/TSS or
    regulation-derived candidates still require a mechanism-specific experiment;
    observed AIS/activity or prey evidence is preferred when it measures the
    hypothesized process directly.

[ ] Require paired rolling-origin evaluation against the identical base model,
    cells, labels, origins, and folds before retaining a governance covariate.
    Record negative results and do not publish unused model projections.


Protected Areas
---------------

[ ] Define an overlap-preserving designation ontology: protected area, marine
    refuge/OECM, sanctuary, wildlife refuge, critical habitat, and other
    conservation designation remain separate types with native authority and
    protection purpose.


Critical Habitat - P0
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Path: ``protected_areas/critical_habitat/``

[ ] Acquire NOAA West Coast and Alaska ESA critical-habitat geometry and DFO
    aquatic SARA critical-habitat geometry for the full AOI, with the final
    rule/recovery document and explicit exclusions for each species or population
    segment.

[ ] Publish species/population-specific source geometry and identifiers; keep
    proposed, final, excluded, and unmapped legal descriptions distinct rather
    than emitting one presence flag or precomputed model-grid fraction.

[ ] Verify legal descriptions against the GIS representation and flag source
    geometries that are general guidance, omit exclusions, or cannot reproduce
    the controlling text.

[ ] Keep critical-habitat fields out of the default model matrix pending an
    explicit circularity and designation-date leakage analysis.

[ ] Publish refuge/NWA ID, authority, designation type, and normalized boundary.

County and Regional Boundaries - P2
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Path: ``jurisdiction/county_regional_boundaries/``

[ ] Acquire current Census TIGER/Line county-equivalent boundaries for every U.S.
    state intersecting the coastal full AOI and official Canadian/B.C. regional

[ ] Decide whether source polygons legally include marine water. Where they do

Tribal and First Nations Areas - P1
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Path: ``jurisdiction/tribal_first_nations_areas/``

[ ] Separate reservations/trust lands, treaty areas, usual-and-accustomed fishing
    areas, modern treaty settlement lands, asserted traditional territories, and
    co-management areas. These concepts are not interchangeable jurisdictions.

[ ] Evaluate BIA/Census land representations and CIRNAC modern-treaty data only
    for the concepts they actually encode. Obtain Nation-published or approved
    sources and consultation guidance for fishing or traditional-use areas.

[ ] Document sensitivity, consent, redistribution, precision, and dispute rules
    before acquisition. Do not derive boundaries from community points, names,
    or third-party generalized maps.

[ ] Publish native area geometry/type, Nation, source/consent status, and
    legal/interpretive caveat; keep unsupported or sensitive geometry out of
    public artifacts.

[ ] Build an as-of-date resolver that combines base regulations with later

Suggested implementation order
------------------------------

[ ] Phase 1: shared contracts, full-AOI source/coverage matrix, deterministic
    ``full_area`` slicing, international/federal/state jurisdiction, fisheries
    management-area geometry, catalog skeleton, and the consolidated governance
    inspection-map framework.

[ ] Phase 2: critical habitat, seasonal closures, salmon zones, TSS, exclusion,
    speed-restriction, and whale-approach rule history with as-of-date queries.

[ ] Phase 3: shipping lanes, gear/harvest rules, MPAs, conservation designations,
    and Tribal/First Nations areas after consent and semantic review.

[ ] Phase 4: refuges, counties/regions, ports, Coast Guard sectors,
    management regions, and reporting areas.

[ ] Before calling the governance domain complete, run a clean local rebuild,
    verify every catalog path against canonical artifacts, inspect cross-border
    maps and temporal rule snapshots, and record all partial/blocked source
    coverage. Verify that the master map contains every implemented catalog layer
    clipped to the configured ``full_area`` and no more than the allowed companion
    maps. Model-projection artifacts are not a governance release gate.
